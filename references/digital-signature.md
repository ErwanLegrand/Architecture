# Digital signature

A scheme that binds a message to a signer's private key so that any holder of the corresponding public key can verify its authenticity and integrity, while no party without the private key can forge one. This framework relies on it to attest a model's identity and to sign audit records through a path outside an agent's authority. The scheme's construction and security analysis belong to cryptography engineering and are defined in that knowledge base.
